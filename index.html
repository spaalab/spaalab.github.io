Research Areas

Our research interests span efficient computing architectures, signal processing, and hardware-oriented AI optimization.

AI/ML Hardware Accelerators

Hardware architectures for efficient machine learning and AI workloads.

Edge Computing

Computing architectures for AI inference at the edge.

Deep Learning Accelerators

High-performance architectures for deep learning computation.

Inference Optimization

Model and computation optimization for efficient AI inference.

Speech Processing

Speech processing, enhancement, and related signal processing techniques.

VLSI and Digital Signal Processing

Digital hardware design and signal processing architectures.

Energy-Efficient Computing

Architectural approaches to improving computing efficiency.

Low-Latency AI Accelerators

Hardware approaches targeting efficient, low-latency AI workloads.

LLM Optimization

Optimization methods for large language model inference and deployment.
