About SPAA Lab

Efficient intelligence, from algorithms to hardware.

SPAA Lab at the Indian Institute of Technology Dharwad works at the intersection of artificial intelligence, computer architecture, VLSI systems, signal processing, and efficient computing.

The lab focuses on developing efficient hardware accelerators, edge computing solutions, deep learning systems, inference optimization techniques, and intelligent computing architectures.

SPAA Lab

The laboratory brings together algorithmic innovation and hardware design to address the growing computational demands of modern AI systems.

Research Areas

Our Research Focus

SPAA Lab explores efficient AI systems across hardware, algorithms, signal processing, and machine learning.

01

Edge Computing Accelerators

Hardware acceleration approaches for machine learning and AI applications at the edge.

02

High-Performance Deep Learning

Efficient architectures and systems for accelerating modern deep learning workloads.

03

AI / ML Accelerators

VLSI and hardware architectures designed for efficient artificial intelligence computing.

04

Inference Model Optimization

Techniques for efficient inference and optimization of machine learning models.

05

Speech Processing

Speech enhancement, noise reduction, feature processing, and deep-learning-based speech systems.

06

Digital Signal Processing

Algorithms and hardware architectures for efficient digital signal processing.
