About SPAA Lab

From algorithms to hardware.

SPAA Lab is a research group at IIT Dharwad working at the intersection of artificial intelligence, computer architecture, and VLSI systems.

We investigate methods that enable powerful AI models to run efficiently under real-world hardware and resource constraints.

Our focus

Efficient AI systems require both algorithmic innovation and hardware-aware design. Our research connects these two layers to build practical and scalable solutions.
