About the Lab

From algorithms to hardware.

The Signal Processing and AI Accelerators (SPAA) Lab at IIT Dharwad focuses on research in AI hardware, deep learning accelerators, inference optimization, speech processing, and digital signal processing.

The lab explores hardware-aware approaches to efficient computing, connecting algorithmic methods with practical digital hardware design.

Research focus

Efficient AI systems, high-performance accelerators, model optimization, and signal processing.
