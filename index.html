About SPAA Lab

From algorithms to hardware.

SPAA Lab is a research group at the Indian Institute of Technology Dharwad working at the intersection of artificial intelligence, computer architecture, and VLSI systems.

The lab investigates efficient AI algorithms, hardware accelerators, transformer optimization, quantization, and hardware–software co-design.

Hardware Meets Intelligence

Modern AI requires both algorithmic innovation and efficient computing hardware. SPAA Lab connects these layers to develop scalable, practical, and intelligent computing systems.
